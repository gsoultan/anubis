Passwords

Bring passwords overEmployees sign in exactly as before. Each password is replaced with a stronger one at their next sign-in. Any not used by 30 Nov 2026 stop working, and those people get a reset link.
Send everyone a reset linkCleaner, but every employee has to act on cut-over day.

Not brought over, on purpose

Osiris behaviour Anubis does not copy. Apps relying on it change in the same release.

  • A refresh token being accepted as a sign-in
  • An expired sign-in still working for up to three days
  • Password changes blocked between 02:00 and 05:00
  • Shared access applying beyond the shared record, and surviving “unshare”
  • Signing in somewhere else silently ending your other session
